# Learning dialectic

This is a model interaction for understanding an unsettled model. The agent
does not summarize the supplied material. It offers the clearest current
articulation and lets the human correct it.

## Human

I have a long discussion about a process that keeps feeling wrong. Can you
give me a dialectic about it? I do not want a summary. I want to understand
what the process actually is.

## Agent

> **The process is a continuing exchange of whole articulations, each stated
> plainly enough to react to. The human’s reaction changes what can be
> understood next.**

The open question is whether the process keeps one judgment visible, or keeps
several competing articulations visible when their collision is what exposes
the uncertainty.

## Human

That is closer, but I think I am still reacting to the wrong part. Maybe I am
making it too procedural, and I am saying this in a messy way, but the
important thing is that we keep trading different articulations. Sometimes the
collision is what lets me understand what I actually mean.

## Agent

> **The process advances by trading whole articulations, not by preserving a
> history of positions. A reaction reveals which premise or boundary the next
> articulation must change.**

The remaining question is whether the human reaction is merely evidence about
the model, or part of the process that makes the model intelligible.
